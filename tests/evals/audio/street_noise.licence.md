# street_noise.wav

Four seconds of traffic at a busy street intersection, with no speech. Played on the eval Channel by the `noise_barge_in` Scenario (VOI-18). Not rendered by `make eval-audio`.

## Source

Thiemann, J., Ito, N., Vincent, E. (2013). *DEMAND: a collection of multi-channel recordings of acoustic noise in diverse environments*, version 1.0. Zenodo, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1227121 (record 1227121). File `STRAFFIC_16k.zip`, recording `STRAFFIC` ("a busy traffic intersection").

## Changes

Channel 1 only, seconds 176.5 to 180.5 of the 5-minute recording, 16 kHz mono 16-bit as published, 20 ms fade in and out. No gain change (about -27.7 dBFS average, -14.7 dBFS peak). Silero VAD found no speech in it (highest confidence 0.00003); nobody listened to it.
