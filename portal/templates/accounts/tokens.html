Access tokens

An Access token lets an AI assistant you use, such as Claude, work on your Voice for you. It works for your Business only. A read-only token can look but not change anything; a full token can do what you can do in the Portal, though some changes, such as removing a Module, wait for your approval, in the chat or under Pending changes. Revoke a token and it stops working at once.
