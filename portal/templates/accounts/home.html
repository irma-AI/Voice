The example call, in words

An animated ring shows one example call as it happens. Time fills the ring, the words run inside it, and each step the agent takes appears on it as a dot.

  1. Step: Caller. Call answered.
  2. Caller: Hi, I have a cut on Thursday at ten.
  3. Caller: Could I move it to Friday?
  4. Step: Instructions. Opening hours read.
  5. Agent: Of course. Let me look at Friday.
  6. Step: Agent. Moving a booking.
  7. Step: MCP · Bookings. Friday 14:00 free.
  8. Agent: Friday at two is free. Shall I?
  9. Caller: Yes, please.
  10. Step: MCP · Bookings. Booking moved.
  11. Agent: Done. Friday at two.
  12. Caller: Could I talk to someone about a gift card?
  13. Step: Human. Hand-off to a person, which is planned and not built yet.
  14. Agent: Of course. Putting you through now.

Answers, acts, connects.

It answers from what you tell it, right on your website. Add Modules and it acts while the caller is on the line. Here is what works today, what a Module adds, and what comes next.
